Features

Everything nutrx
can do.

A privacy-first nutrient tracker that works across your iPhone, Apple Watch, home screen, and lock screen. Here's what you get.

Apple Watch

New

A focused wrist companion for logging intake. See today's active nutrients with goal-type-aware progress bars and log one step increment with a single tap. Reads from the same data store as the iPhone app — no extra setup, data appears automatically.

One-tap logging Watch complications Shared data store Ring gauge & inline text

iCloud Sync

All your data syncs automatically across every Apple device via CloudKit. On by default, no setup required. Works offline — sync is additive, never a dependency. Reinstalling the app or setting up a new device restores everything from iCloud automatically.

Automatic sync New-device restore Works offline Toggle on/off anytime

Widgets

Six widget configurations across home screen, lock screen, and watch face. Interactive + buttons let you log intake without opening the app. Small, medium, and large sizes adapt to show 2, 3, or 6 nutrients. Lock screen widgets show a completion ring gauge or inline progress text.

Home screen (S/M/L) Lock screen Interactive logging Instant refresh

Analytics & Charts

Tap any nutrient to see a dedicated analytics screen with three cards: a daily intake bar chart across 7, 30, or 90 days; period stats with hit rate, daily average, and target; and day-of-week patterns highlighting your strongest day. Built with Swift Charts — native, fast, and beautiful.

Daily intake chart Hit rate & averages Day-of-week patterns 7D / 30D / 90D range

Streaks & Consistency

Track how many consecutive days you've hit all your targets. Your streak is visible on the Today screen, in History with flame indicators on streak days, and on home screen widgets. A streak day counts when every active nutrient meets its goal — respecting exclusions, creation dates, and goal types.

Current & best streak Flame badges in History Widget streak count Goal-type aware

And plenty more.

Every detail designed to make daily tracking effortless.

Goal types

Set each nutrient as "at least" (minimum), "at most" (maximum), or "between" (range). Progress bars, charts, streaks, and widgets all adapt to the selected type.

Dose reminders

Set multiple daily reminders per nutrient. Smart suppression cancels upcoming reminders after you log intake so you're never nagged twice.

Nutrient groups

Organise nutrients into collapsible categories like Vitamins, Minerals, or Supplements. Groups show aggregate progress when collapsed.

Fully customisable

No preset list. Define your own nutrients with custom names, units, step sizes, and daily targets. Track anything you want, any way you want.

Expandable cards

Tap any nutrient on Today to see every intake logged that day with timestamps, amounts, and notes in a chronological breakdown.

Full history

Browse every past day at a glance with monthly section headers. Every intake entry is stored locally and nothing is ever deleted.

Swipe actions

Swipe right on any nutrient to add an exact amount with optional notes. Swipe left to jump straight to editing.

Profile & photo

Set your name, birthday, weight, height, and a profile photo from your library or camera. Your photo appears in the nav bar across all tabs.

Private by design

No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads, no third-party SDKs. Your health data never leaves your device.
