Privacy Policy

We collect nothing.

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

No data collected. Your data stays on your devices and, with iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud. It never reaches us.
No accounts. You never create an account or provide any personal information to us.
No analytics. We have no telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking of any kind.
No ads. nutrx is not ad-supported and never will be.
No third-party SDKs. There are no third-party libraries or services embedded in the app.
Apple Health stays on your iPhone. If you connect it, nutrx only reads, never writes, and never stores or syncs what it reads.
On-device intelligence only. Label reading and recap wording run on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

Overview

nutrx is a privacy-first iOS application for tracking daily nutrient intake. This policy explains how we handle your information — which, to be direct, we do not handle at all.

nutrx is built by nutrx Labs. We believe your health data is yours and yours alone. The app is designed from the ground up so that your data never reaches us: there is no nutrx server, account or analytics service for it to go to.

Data we collect

None. nutrx does not collect any personal data, health data, usage data, or any other information from you or your device.

Everything you enter into nutrx — your nutrients, daily logs, notes, reminders, optional profile details and photo, and history — is stored in the app's database on your iPhone or iPad, and shared with nutrx's widgets and Apple Watch app on your own devices. It is never accessible to us or any third party.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, nutrx syncs your data across your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), in your own private iCloud database. It's on by default, and you can turn it off at any time in nutrx under Settings → iCloud Sync. Your data is stored and protected by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to your private iCloud database and can't see what's in it.

Apple Health

nutrx Pro can show how your intake lines up with your sleep, workouts and active energy. This is optional and off until you turn it on in nutrx. If you connect Apple Health, nutrx asks iOS for permission to read three things: sleep analysis, workouts and active energy. It never writes anything to Apple Health.

What nutrx reads is used on your iPhone, in memory, to work out the patterns it shows you. It is never saved in nutrx's database, never synced to iCloud, never shown in widgets, and never sent to us or anyone else. The only thing nutrx remembers is whether you switched the connection on, on that device. You can disconnect in nutrx under Settings → Apple Health, and change or remove nutrx's access at any time in the Health app.

On-device intelligence

Two nutrx Pro features use Apple Intelligence where your iPhone supports it: reading a supplement label you scan, and wording your weekly recap. Both run entirely on your device with Apple's on-device model. Nothing you scan or log is sent to an AI service, Apple's or anyone else's. Where Apple Intelligence isn't available, nutrx uses simpler text matching and templates on the device instead.

Camera and photos

nutrx uses the camera only when you choose to take a profile photo or scan a label, and reads a photo from your library only when you pick one. A scanned label is read on your iPhone to fill in the new-nutrient form, and the image isn't kept. A profile photo is stored with your profile, on your devices and in your private iCloud when sync is on.

Purchases

nutrx Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. nutrx checks whether you have Pro on your device each time it starts, using Apple's StoreKit, and doesn't store that result in its database or in iCloud.

Files you export

Export data (a CSV of your log) and the report for your doctor (a PDF, nutrx Pro) are made on your device and shared only where you choose in the share sheet. nutrx doesn't keep a copy: the file is removed from the device when the share sheet closes.

Internet access

nutrx doesn't need an internet connection. It works entirely offline. Its only network traffic goes through Apple: iCloud syncing your data when sync is on, and the App Store for nutrx Pro purchases and when you choose to rate nutrx. Reminders are scheduled on your device. There is no nutrx backend server, API, or cloud service.

Analytics and crash reporting

nutrx contains no analytics, telemetry, event tracking, or crash reporting tools. We have no visibility into how you use the app, how often you open it, or whether it crashes. If you encounter a bug, please reach out to us directly via the support page.

Third-party services

nutrx contains no third-party SDKs, advertising frameworks, or external services. The only software running in the app is code we wrote ourselves, built on Apple's native iOS frameworks.

Data deletion

You are in full control of your data at all times. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. If you use iCloud sync, a copy also stays in your private iCloud until you delete it: in nutrx, go to Settings → iCloud Sync → Delete iCloud Data, or remove nutrx's data in your device's iCloud storage settings. There is no account to close, no server-side data to request deletion of, and no one to contact — because we never had your data to begin with.

Children's privacy

nutrx does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected by us or sent to us, nutrx is compliant with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how nutrx handles data, we will update this policy and clearly communicate the change before it takes effect. The current version date is always shown at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how nutrx works, you can reach us at support@nutrx-labs.com. We're a small team and we read every message.
