v2.1 16 Oct 2026 Current

Features

Stacks

Log everything you take together in one tap. Make a "Morning" stack with Vitamin D, Omega-3 and Magnesium and log it from its own Stacks tab or from Today. Change the amounts for that time, undo it, and see which stacks you've logged today.

Siri and Shortcuts

Say "Log my Vitamin D with nutrx" or "Log my Morning stack with nutrx", or use the new Log Nutrient and Log Stack actions in the Shortcuts app. nutrx answers with your new total.

Export your data

Settings → Export data saves your log as a CSV file, one row per entry, for all time or the last 30 or 90 days. It opens in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets, and it's made on your iPhone.

nutrx Pro: Supply & refills

An optional upgrade, new in 2.1. Tell nutrx how many servings are in the bottle and it counts down as you log, warns on Today when you're running low, and sends one refill reminder per bottle.

nutrx Pro: Scan a label

Point the camera at a bottle's Supplement Facts and the new-nutrient form fills itself in. Read on your iPhone, with Apple Intelligence where available; nothing is uploaded, and the goal is always yours to set.

nutrx Pro: Weekly recap

Every Sunday evening, a short summary of your week: goals met, full days, your steadiest nutrient, what slipped and the weekday you tend to miss. It describes your week and never gives advice.

nutrx Pro: Trends with Apple Health

See how your intake lines up with your sleep, workouts and active energy over four weeks. nutrx only reads from Health, nothing it reads is stored or synced, and it shows patterns, not causes.

nutrx Pro: Report for your doctor

A clean PDF of any period to bring to an appointment: each nutrient's goal, average, hit rate and days logged, a chart of daily totals and your notes. From History or Settings.

Improvements

Everything free stays free

nutrx Pro only adds things around tracking. Logging, history, streaks, analytics, widgets, the Watch, iCloud sync, stacks, Siri and export are free, with no ads.
