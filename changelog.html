What's New

Every update,
documented.

nutrx ships fast and improves constantly. Here's everything that's changed, version by version.

v2.0 Oct 2026 Current

Features

A redesigned Today screen

A day-score card shows how many goals you've met, your streak and your next reminder. Every nutrient gets a progress ring in its own colour and icon, a clear status line ("2,000 IU to go", "80 mg left today", "Done") and bigger − / + buttons. Light and dark mode are both fully styled.

Log sheet

Tap a nutrient to log an exact amount: step it up or down, pick ½×, 1× or 2× a step or "Fill to goal", add a note, and see everything you logged today. Delete any entry with one tap.

Colours, icons and goal cards

Pick a colour and an icon for each nutrient, choose "At least", "At most" or "Between" from simple cards, and watch a live preview of how it will look on Today as you type.

Redesigned analytics

Hit rate, daily average and best day for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, a bar chart in the nutrient's colour, and a weekday heatmap of the last four weeks that points out the day you tend to miss.

Redesigned History

A streak card with your current and best streak, a month calendar coloured by how many goals you met each day, and your recent days with a dot per nutrient. Tap a day for every total and entry.

New welcome and first nutrient

Pick a starting point — Vitamin D, Magnesium, Omega-3, Water, Protein or Creatine — and enter your own goal and step, or make your own. nutrx doesn't suggest amounts, and you can skip and add nutrients later.

Redesigned widgets, plus Next up

A small widget with your day at a glance on nutrx green, a quick-log widget with each nutrient's ring and a + button, and a new "Next up" Lock Screen widget with the next nutrient you still need more of.

Redesigned Apple Watch app and complications

Today with your goals met at the top and a ring per nutrient. Logging shows your new total for a moment, with a stronger tap when you reach a goal. New complications: a segmented ring, a curved gauge, a rectangular bar and an inline count with your streak.

Improvements

No personal details needed

Setup no longer asks for your name, birthday, weight or height. They're all optional and live in Edit Profile.

History counts every nutrient

A day's score includes nutrients you didn't log at all, so a day where you skipped one no longer looks complete.

One rule for "goal met"

Today, History, streaks, analytics, widgets and the Watch share a single goal check, so they can't disagree.

VoiceOver, larger text and Reduce Motion

Every control and field has a spoken name, layouts stack at the largest text sizes, and nothing slides with Reduce Motion on.

A different tap when you hit a goal

Logging that reaches a goal gives a success haptic, on iPhone as on the Watch.

Fixes

Reminders after logging

Logging a nutrient now silences only the rest of that day's reminders for it, and later days keep theirs. Logs from the widget, the Watch or another device count too.

No freeze when notifications are slow

Right after a restart iOS's notification service can take minutes to answer; nutrx no longer waits for it as it opens.

Quick-log widget +

Tapping + on the quick-log widget no longer crashes the widget.

The keyboard covering fields

The nutrient form scrolls the field you're typing in above the keyboard, and setup keeps Start tracking clear of it, at every text size.
