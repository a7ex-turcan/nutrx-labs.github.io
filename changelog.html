Features

Onboarding

A two-step setup flow — personal info first, then your first nutrient. The app is never empty on day one.

User-defined nutrients

No preset list. Create any nutrient with a custom name, unit, step size, and daily target.

Today, My Nutrients & History tabs

The core three-tab layout. Log intake with +/−, manage your list, and browse every past day.

Fully offline & private

All data stored locally via SwiftData. No accounts, no servers, no telemetry — ever.
