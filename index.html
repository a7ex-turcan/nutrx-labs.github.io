Why nutrx

Built for real life. Not for engagement.

Private by design

Your data lives on your devices and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud. No accounts, no nutrx servers, no analytics.

Fully customisable

Your own nutrients, units, step sizes, goal types and groups. Track anything, the way you actually take it.

Works offline

No connection needed — ever. Sync is a bonus, never a requirement.

Full history

Every day, every entry, kept on your device. Nothing is deleted automatically.

Free forever

All core tracking stays free. A Pro tier will only add AI-powered extras.

One tap to log

Tap + to log a step, or open a nutrient to add an exact amount with a note. Logging takes seconds.

Packed with features

Simple on the surface. Powerful underneath.

iCloud sync

Across your iPhone, iPad and Watch, through your own private iCloud.

Apple Watch

Log from your wrist in a tap. Complications keep your rings on your face.

Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen. See progress and log without opening the app.

Streaks

See how consistent you are, on Today, in History and on your widgets.

Analytics

Per-nutrient charts: daily intake, hit rate, averages and weekday patterns.

Goal types

At least, at most, or between. Rings and charts adapt automatically.

Reminders

Per-nutrient dose reminders that go quiet once you've logged.

Groups

Organise nutrients into collapsible groups like Morning stack or Through the day.

nutrx Pro · Schedules new in 2.4

A little more, if you want it.

An optional upgrade that adds a few things around tracking. Everything else in nutrx stays free, including stacks, Siri and data export, and nothing free will ever move behind it.

Supply & refills

Know when a bottle runs out. nutrx counts down as you log and reminds you once, a week ahead.

Scan a label

Point the camera at the Supplement Facts and the new nutrient fills itself in. Read on your iPhone.

Weekly recap

Every Sunday, your week in a few lines: goals met, your steadiest nutrient, what slipped.

Trends with Apple Health

How your intake lines up with sleep and workouts. Patterns in your own data, never advice.

Report for your doctor

A clean PDF of any period: goals, daily totals, hit rates and your notes.

Courses

Taking something for 5 days, or until a date? It leaves Today after the last day, and your streak isn't affected.

Schedules

Take something on set days: weekdays, every few days, on and off, or a few times a week. On its days off it waits under Not today, and those days never count as missed.
